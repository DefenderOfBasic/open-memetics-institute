# 🛠️ Tools & Resources

- [Postscope](https://postscope.pages.dev/) extracts tweets on a given page and visualizes them as a discourse map. 
- [Good & Evil Words / Semantic Embeddings Viewer](https://github.com/DefenderOfBasic/good-and-evil-concepts?tab=readme-ov-file#polarized-words) allows you to query the "collective consciousness", essentially a kind of survey of the aggregate population.
